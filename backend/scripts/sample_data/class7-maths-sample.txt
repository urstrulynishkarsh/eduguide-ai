# Chapter 4 Simple Equations

An equation is a balance. Whatever we do to one side, we must do to the other side so that
both sides stay equal. In this chapter we learn to write equations from everyday situations
and to solve them step by step.

## 4.1 What Is an Equation?
A statement such as x + 3 = 8 is an equation. It has a variable (x), an equals sign and two
sides. The left side is x + 3 and the right side is 8. A value of x that makes both sides equal
is called the solution of the equation. Here the solution is x = 5, because 5 + 3 = 8.

Example 1: Write an equation for "a number increased by 7 gives 15".
Solution: Let the number be n. Increasing it by 7 gives n + 7. So the equation is n + 7 = 15.

## 4.2 Solving an Equation
To solve an equation we undo what has been done to the variable. If a number was added, we
subtract it from both sides. If the variable was multiplied by a number, we divide both sides
by that number.

Example 2: Solve 2x + 4 = 10.
Solution: Subtract 4 from both sides: 2x = 6. Divide both sides by 2: x = 3.
Check: 2 × 3 + 4 = 10. The left side equals the right side, so x = 3 is correct.

## 4.3 Equations in Daily Life
Equations help us answer questions about money, age and measurement.

Example 3: Riya has some pencils. Her friend gives her 6 more and now she has 20. How many
pencils did Riya have at first?
Solution: Let the number of pencils be p. Then p + 6 = 20. Subtract 6 from both sides: p = 14.
Riya had 14 pencils at first.

## What have we discussed?
An equation states that two expressions are equal. To solve it, do the same operation on both
sides until the variable is alone on one side. Always check the answer by putting it back into
the equation.
